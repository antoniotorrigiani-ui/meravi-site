The studio

Between sculpture and technology

Meravi.Art was born from a simple idea: every space deserves a piece with a story. Each work starts as a pencil sketch, becomes a precise 3D model, is printed in sections and then sanded, assembled and painted by hand — layer after layer, until it looks carved rather than printed.

We create small gifts and pieces several metres tall: portraits of your pet, monumental sculptures for showrooms and hotels, illuminated logos and signage for brands, decorative elements designed around your interior. Glossy lacquer, metallic, mirror, marble or matte finishes — in any colour.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom piece cost?

Pet portraits start from €50, business logos from €300. Large installations are quoted individually based on size, finish and installation — the quote is free and without obligation.

How long does it take?

Collection pieces ship in a few days. Custom works take 2–6 weeks depending on size and complexity.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes, throughout Europe, with protective packaging. Shipping is free for orders over €100; large pieces are shipped with dedicated couriers.

How does payment work for custom orders?

A deposit to start the project after you approve the 3D render, and the balance before shipping.

What material are the sculptures made of?

High-quality 3D-printed polymer, reinforced, sanded and finished with primers and professional lacquers: lightweight, strong and with a ceramic-like surface.
